### Whimsical World
- Definition：The second half of the scroll-driven narrative after the camera dives through the monitor — a stylized 3D environment where the CV content (About, Projects, Skills, Experience, Education, Contact) is presented as interactive panels instead of a traditional website layout.

### Story Content
- Definition：The overlay UI layer (`StoryContent.jsx`) that fades in and out as the user scrolls through the 700vh scroll track, displaying the portfolio sections pulled from `lib/data.js`.

### Circuit Garden
- Definition：The visual presentation style used for the Skills section within the Whimsical World, framing skills as a garden-like circuit diagram rather than a plain list.

### Emaan Avatar
- Definition：The blocky, pixel-style 3D character representing Emaan Fatima, built procedurally in Three.js with curly brown hair, pink top with pixel heart, blue jeans, and white shoes; it waves at the start, walks to the chair, sits down, and rests its arms on the desk.
