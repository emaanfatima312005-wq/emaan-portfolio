GSAP plugins must be registered before use — `ScrollTrigger` is registered at the top of `ScrollStory.jsx`, `StoryContent.jsx`, `ThreeWorkspace.jsx`, and `SmoothScroll.jsx` via `gsap.registerPlugin(ScrollTrigger)`; `ThreeWorkspace` also registers it inside the component body. Smooth scrolling requires Lenis to be mounted once (via `SmoothScroll`) so that `ScrollTrigger.update` stays in sync with Lenis's scroll events.
